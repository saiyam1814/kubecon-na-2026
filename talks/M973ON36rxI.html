Talk summary

Marcin Wielgus, Amy Chen and Yuki Iwai give an update on the Kubernetes Batch Working Group and its projects. They cover new Kueue features such as concurrent admission, configurable preemption, topology-aware spreading, the dynamic quota orchestrator and partial scale-up, then Job, JobSet and workload-aware scheduling updates.

Watch this if:

you run AI training or batch jobs on Kubernetes and want to know what changed in Kueue, Job and JobSet.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 3:46 Wielgus says that since last November Kueue shipped 54 releases, while PRs, issues and contributors each more than doubled.
  2. 4:45 Wielgus explains concurrent admission, where virtual copies of a workload keep trying for cheaper reserved capacity and move the job there once it frees up.
  3. 12:36 Wielgus introduces topology-aware spreading for inference, which caps the share of serving capacity placed in one rack, for example at 45%.
  4. 14:27 Chen describes the dynamic quota orchestrator, which discovers real capacity from node pools or MultiKueue worker clusters and adjusts nominal quota automatically.
  5. 28:54 Iwai explains that Kueue is adopting a shared scheduler library from kube-scheduler so its node feasibility checks match what kube-scheduler will accept.
