Talk summary

Cortney Nickerson and Jim Bugwadia open KyvernoCon in under three minutes, running through an agenda they say leans toward AI, GPUs and guardrails. Bugwadia briefly restates Kyverno's purpose, and Nickerson points to a ContribFest session the next day.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:19 Cortney Nickerson says the agenda has quite a few AI talks, covering AI workload placement at scale, GPUs, building guardrails, and Envoy AI Gateway with Kyverno.
  2. 1:10 She says Kyverno graduated in March.
  3. 1:39 Jim Bugwadia says Kyverno started in Kubernetes and has evolved beyond it.
  4. 1:45 He describes its purpose as governance for clusters, infrastructure and, increasingly, AI applications at scale.
  5. 2:15 Nickerson announces a Kyverno ContribFest session the next day, where maintainers will take questions on installations, policies and the pending deprecation that changes policy types.
