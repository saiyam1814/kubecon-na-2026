Talk summary

The speaker runs a five-minute live demo of Istio traffic management features, sending 100 requests through each scenario. He shows weighted canary and blue-green routing, header-based routing, traffic mirroring, and fault injection.

Watch this if:

You want a quick live tour of Istio canary, blue-green, header routing, mirroring and fault injection.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:09 The speaker starts with a canary scenario, setting route weights and sending 100 requests to show the split between two versions.
  2. 1:25 He shows a blue-green scenario where all traffic goes to one version and promotion is done by changing the weight.
  3. 2:04 He demonstrates header-based routing, with requests split by three exact header values.
  4. 2:48 He demonstrates mirroring, where live traffic to the real target is copied to a second target, which he says helps test a new version with production traffic.
  5. 3:44 He demonstrates fault injection with 20 percent errors and a one-second delay, and says it helps show how an application handles failures.
