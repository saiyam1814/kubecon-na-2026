Talk summary

William Crum and Daniel Moch introduce the Public Sector User Group as a way for cloud-native practitioners in regulated environments to compare problems and share feedback. Attendees discuss borrowing approaches across industries, getting agreement between teams, and making it easier to share software without creating unintended support obligations.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 1:19 Crum describes starting a community around recurring problems such as air-gapped image loading and says the group can bring feedback from public-sector users to CNCF advisory groups.
  2. 19:31 A speaker argues that public-sector teams can learn from other regulated industries, pointing to self-hosted registries for air-gapped Kubernetes as an example they do not need to reinvent.
  3. 20:12 An attendee says modernizing a distributed process is difficult when several groups own different steps and all need to support the same platform direction.
  4. 24:54 An attendee describes plans for separate core, community, and verified-publisher areas in Iron Bank as a way to make it easier for more groups to contribute.
  5. 27:12 An attendee recounts how an internal RPM mirror became a de facto shared service and recommends spelling out support commitments before encouraging others to rely on it.
