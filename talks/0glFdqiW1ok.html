Talk summary

The hosts open the summit by celebrating ten years of KubeVirt and its recent graduation. They thank the maintainers and explain where the project could use more contributors.

Watch this if:

You want a quick introduction to the KubeVirt community and where it needs contributors.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:17 The hosts celebrate ten years of KubeVirt and say the project graduated the week before the summit.
  2. 0:59 The first host thanks the maintainers and shows two slides recognizing 149 people who have approved changes across the KubeVirt repositories.
  3. 2:02 The first host says the enhancement-proposal backlog is the largest it has been and asks for help reviewing proposals.
  4. 2:17 The first host names concrete ways to help, including filing issues, opening pull requests, working on releases and backports, and lending security expertise.
  5. 3:14 The hosts invite KubeVirt users to visit the project kiosk at 2 p.m. to celebrate with the community.
