Talk summary

The co-chairs open Istio Day, recap what they say the project shipped over the last year, and list roadmap items for the next. They ask attendees to tell the project what they want prioritized and explain how to get involved.

Watch this if:

You want a four-minute recap of what Istio shipped in the last year and what its maintainers plan next.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:59 The co-chair says Istio made four releases over the last year, with the latest a few days before, focused on functionality, compatibility and security fixes.
  2. 1:29 The co-chair lists ambient mesh improvements, including the promotion of multi-cluster for ambient and work on migration from sidecar to ambient.
  3. 1:45 The co-chair lists AI features such as the Gateway API Inference Extension promotion and agentgateway support, plus security and Gateway API compatibility changes.
  4. 1:57 The co-chair gives the roadmap for next year, including wildcard service entry support, better Envoy and ztunnel reliability and agentgateway promotion.
  5. 2:25 The co-chair asks attendees to say which features they want prioritized and points to Slack, working groups and responsible bug reports.
