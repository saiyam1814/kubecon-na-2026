Talk summary

The hosts open EnvoyCon, which they describe as marking Envoy's 10-year anniversary, and explain that it runs as seven 10-minute lightning talks with no question time. They read out the running order and describe Envoy as a growing family of projects.

Watch this if:

You want a three-minute overview of the EnvoyCon lightning talks before picking which ones to watch.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:29 Rohit Agrawal says this EnvoyCon marks 10 years of Envoy and that he started working on it in 2019.
  2. 0:56 He explains the format: seven talks of 10 minutes each, no Q&A, with follow-up questions at the Envoy booth.
  3. 1:13 He says people can stay in touch after KubeCon through the project's Slack or by opening a GitHub thread.
  4. 1:31 He reads the running order, from the agentic networking stack through dynamic modules, Envoy Gateway in the enterprise, ListenerSets, GEICO's bridge, request queuing and Envoy in Cilium.
  5. 2:26 Erica Hughberg describes Envoy as a family: Envoy proxy, then Envoy Gateway as a control plane for Kubernetes, and Agent Router, which she says was formerly Envoy AI Gateway and builds on both.
