Talk summary

Four members of Kubernetes SIG Contributor Experience walk through the SIG's subprojects as they are being reorganized, covering contributor communications, events, new contributor orientation, elections, GitHub management, mentoring and community infrastructure, with statistics on the contributor base and the project's social media accounts. They also explain the project's AI usage disclosure policy and the review burden that led to it, and take questions.

Watch this if:

you want to start contributing to Kubernetes or want to understand its policy on AI-assisted contributions.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 1:04 Nabarun Pal gives community statistics, saying Kubernetes has more than 100,000 contributors, the largest contributor base among CNCF projects, and noting the numbers are approximate because of GitHub archive data issues.
  2. 3:09 He presents the planned reorganization of the SIG's subprojects, which he says will be proposed in a couple of weeks, and describes what each one does.
  3. 9:23 Kaslin Fields reports that the project's LinkedIn account gained almost 25,000 followers this year, roughly doubling for the second year in a row, while the X account remains the largest.
  4. 18:19 Priyanka Saggu says an AI usage disclosure template was added to the kubernetes/kubernetes repository, asking contributors to declare how they used AI tools.
  5. 23:57 Pal says the SIG is not anti-AI but wants responsible use and disclosure, citing reviewers flooded with trivial or very large AI-generated pull requests.
