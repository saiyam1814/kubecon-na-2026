Talk summary

Paula Kennedy and Hannah Foxwell explain why Platform Engineering Day was created and orient attendees to the day's program. Kennedy explains how recordings let attendees catch up on the other afternoon track and asks them to discuss how platform engineering should adapt to changes in AI.

Watch this if:

You want the organizers' explanation of why Platform Engineering Day exists and what conversations they hoped to start.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:33 Kennedy introduces herself, thanks the other program co-chairs and the committee, and introduces Foxwell as the co-host who will lead the second afternoon track.
  2. 1:18 The hosts explain how to access captioning and live translation on a personal device, and remind attendees how to report a code-of-conduct concern.
  3. 3:39 Kennedy traces the event to conversations at KubeCon Amsterdam in 2023 about giving platform engineering principles and practitioner experience more space.
  4. 4:43 She asks attendees to discuss how platform engineering should adapt to changes in AI and to tell the co-chairs what they would like covered next.
  5. 5:50 She explains the afternoon split into two tracks and tells attendees that they can catch up on talks they miss using the recordings.
