Talk summary

The chairs and tech leads of the CNCF Technical Advisory Group for Operational Resilience explain the group's charter and walk through its open initiatives, from project release guidelines and scale testing guidance to observability personas, business continuity and AI sustainability papers. They describe how initiatives are proposed and reviewed in the CNCF TOC repository and ask for contributors and reviewers.

Watch this if:

you work on reliability, observability or sustainability and want to see which CNCF operational resilience initiatives are open and where they need help.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 4:12 Alolita Sharma reads the group's charter: to define and advance good practices and standards for resilient, observable and efficient cloud native systems beyond the initial development cycle, which she sums up as production readiness.
  2. 8:17 Nabarun Pal presents the Project Release Guidelines initiative, which studies release patterns across CNCF projects to produce guidelines and reference implementations; he says a white paper draft is pending and needs reviewers.
  3. 10:30 Pal says the TOC asked the group for scale and performance testing guidance to use in its due diligence for incubation and graduation, and that the work is about to start by collecting data from projects.
  4. 11:51 Diana Todea describes an ongoing reference framework for levels of service reliability automation, meant to show how reliability tasks move from manual work toward autonomy.
  5. 15:02 Niki Manoledaki shows the cloud native AI sustainability foundations and framework paper, written with TAG Infrastructure, which she says is almost ready to publish and covers measuring power use with tools like Kepler.
