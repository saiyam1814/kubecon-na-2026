Talk summary

The speakers explain why the Kubernetes working group is moving from scheduling individual pods to scheduling whole workloads. They describe the APIs, controller integrations, and roadmap for bringing placement, preemption, and resource sharing together.

Watch this if:

you maintain a workload controller or want to understand the Kubernetes workload scheduling roadmap.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 2:44 The speakers frame the new APIs around gang scheduling, topology constraints, and preemption at the workload level.
  2. 4:00 They distinguish a PodGroup, which groups pods with shared scheduling requirements, from a CompositePodGroup, which groups other groups.
  3. 8:45 They describe integrations with out-of-tree workload controllers such as JobSet and LeaderWorkerSet.
  4. 20:27 They describe a read-only scheduler library that lets controllers ask what would happen if a workload were placed.
  5. 26:13 They outline a goal of graduating the core APIs and scheduling capabilities in Kubernetes 1.39, with performance and hardening work still ahead.
