Talk summary

Vinicius Krischke describes how Nubank allocates Kubernetes idle cost node by node instead of spreading it across all workloads by their share of requests. He argues that this sends a cost signal to the services that cause idle, and that at large scale the allocation evens out without hand-written fairness rules.

Watch this if:

you allocate Kubernetes costs to teams and want idle cost to reach the services that cause it.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:19 Vinicius Krischke describes the common approach: take the whole month's idle and spread it across services by their share of requests.
  2. 0:57 He explains Nubank's alternative: allocate idle per node, to the pods running on that node.
  3. 1:35 He says this shifts idle cost to the services that generate it and sends a cost signal, while engineers still decide whether a resource is worth it.
  4. 2:31 He takes the fairness objection, a service landing on a node next to a wasteful one, and argues the odds of that repeating are small.
  5. 3:04 He says that as the number of instances grows the allocation approaches the mean, so no complicated rules are needed.
