Talk summary

Panelists from AWS, NVIDIA and Capital One describe why they use and contribute to Kubeflow, covering Spark Operator, distributed training and Kubeflow Pipelines. They then say where they think Kubeflow goes next, from Semantic Operator and agents to keeping a solid set of primitives.

Watch this if:

You want to hear how AWS, NVIDIA and Capital One people use Kubeflow for Spark, training and pipelines, and where they think it is heading.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 6:24 Vara Bonthu recounts how Spark Operator moved from Google into Kubeflow and says it now lists more than 120 public adopters.
  2. 8:36 Ekin Karabulut says that as a data scientist she values launching distributed training through Trainer without worrying about networking or node placement.
  3. 10:30 Michael Zazula says Kubeflow Pipelines unified data science teams at Capital One that had been building in unmanaged namespaces.
  4. 13:38 Vara Bonthu says Semantic Operator is a first step toward agents working with data and getting deterministic answers from metric definitions.
  5. 18:52 A Kubeflow founder invited from the audience urges the community to keep the primitives solid and build clean contracts on top of them.
