Talk summary

In a short opening, Danielle Cook describes Cloud Native University as an introduction to KubeCon and cloud native made of quick sessions meant to guide attendees through the following three days. She and Simon Forster then introduce themselves as CNCF Ambassadors from Akamai.

  1. 0:13 Danielle Cook says Cloud Native University is set up as an intro to what KubeCon and cloud native are.
  2. 0:26 She describes the program as a set of quick, digestible sessions that give a taster of different technologies.
  3. 0:39 She says it is meant to help whether it is someone's first KubeCon or their fourth.
  4. 0:50 She introduces herself as a CNCF Ambassador who co-runs a working group with Simon Forster that publishes and maintains the Cloud Native Maturity Model, and says this is her 19th KubeCon.
