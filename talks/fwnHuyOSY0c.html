Talk summary

Jorge Castro celebrates the people who sustain the cloud native ecosystem and says community is about what contributors stand for together. He presents awards for documentation, behind-the-scenes work, technical contributions, and long-term service.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:06 Castro gives a decade-wide snapshot of contributors, countries, and projects before reflecting on what binds the community together.
  2. 0:39 Castro introduces the Lorem Ipsum award and explains why documentation is both an entry point for contributors and a way to help a community grow.
  3. 1:40 Castro presents the Chop Wood Carry Water award for mentoring and other essential work that often happens out of sight.
  4. 2:51 Castro introduces the Top Committer award as recognition for technical contributions that benefit CNCF projects as a whole.
  5. 3:36 Castro describes the Lifetime Achievement award as recognizing sustained work that shaped cloud native technology and its communities.
