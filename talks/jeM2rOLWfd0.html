Talk summary

Erica Hughberg argues that coding agents need to be able to find and use a developer portal's platform capabilities without a human guide. She proposes comparing what services declare they intend to do with what they actually do at runtime when code ships without a full review.

Watch this if:

You maintain a developer portal and want agents to find the right platform capabilities without inventing their own.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 1:58 Hughberg points out that teams wrote their golden paths, capability catalogs, and integration guides for humans, and asks them to treat coding agents as another audience.
  2. 3:48 She refers to the platform engineering community's work on platform capabilities and asks how teams can steer agent-assisted contributions back into shared tools instead of duplicating them.
  3. 6:47 She advises moving guidance out of chat-only channels and giving agents programmatic ways to find and invoke existing platform capabilities.
  4. 8:44 She proposes having production services declare what they intend to do, so runtime guardrails have something to compare with observed behavior when code was not fully reviewed.
  5. 9:14 She closes with questions teams can ask about their portals: can an agent read the guidance without a human guide, use the right capability, and make mistakes in a sandbox?
