Talk summary

Lucy Sweet, a Kubernetes maintainer at Anthropic, thanks open source maintainers and names Kubernetes, Prometheus and Envoy as foundations Anthropic uses to build and serve its AI. She announces that Anthropic is donating 3,000 codes for a Claude plan to CNCF maintainers, requested through claude.ai/cncf.

Watch this if:

you are a CNCF maintainer and want the details of Anthropic's Claude offer for maintainers.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:06 Lucy Sweet, a Kubernetes maintainer, says open source is special because competitors build their core tools together in the open and give them away.
  2. 0:25 She points to Kubernetes, Prometheus, the second project to join CNCF, and Envoy, which started inside Lyft, as examples of what came out of that.
  3. 1:01 She says the CNCF landscape has hundreds of free projects and that many maintainers do this work unpaid because they believe in it.
  4. 1:23 She says Anthropic builds and serves its AI on these foundations, naming Kubernetes, Prometheus and Envoy.
  5. 1:44 She announces that Anthropic is donating 3,000 codes for a Claude plan to CNCF maintainers, on top of its existing Claude for Open Source program, with access requested at claude.ai/cncf.
