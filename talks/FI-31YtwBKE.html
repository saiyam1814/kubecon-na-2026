Talk summary

Gavrish Prabhu, a KServe maintainer, says plain vLLM pods are not enough for production LLM serving, which also needs routers, proxies and KV cache management. He introduces KServe's LLMInferenceService custom resource for generative AI workloads and outlines its model, router and template components.

Watch this if:

You serve LLMs on Kubernetes and want a short introduction to KServe's LLMInferenceService resource.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:28 Gavrish Prabhu says KServe started with predictive AI as part of the Kubeflow project and that moving to LLMs was a normal transition.
  2. 0:50 He says running plain vLLM pods does not help in production-grade systems, which need routers, proxies, KV cache management and more.
  3. 1:07 He says the long-standing InferenceService CRD is battle tested but not meant for generative AI use cases, which need more templates and worker nodes.
  4. 1:31 He says the new LLMInferenceService CRD is specific to generative AI and can manage single-node and multi-node setups, prefill and decode pods, KV cache routing and gateway configuration.
  5. 2:04 He says LLMInferenceService has three main parts: a model component, a router component for routing, gateway and schedulers, and a template for workers and runtime.
