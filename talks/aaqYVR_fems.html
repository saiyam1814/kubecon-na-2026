Talk summary

The speakers present OptimizationJob, a new Kubeflow Trainer API that they say runs hyperparameter tuning with each trial as a TrainJob and no second system to install. They explain how it grew out of Katib, play a recorded demo, and list the limits of the first alpha phase and the roadmap.

Watch this if:

You tune hyperparameters on Kubernetes and want to know what the new OptimizationJob API in Kubeflow Trainer does today and what it leaves out.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 3:13 The speaker says Katib works but means two installs, two APIs and metrics that live outside the job.
  2. 3:51 The speaker says the change that made this possible was putting training metrics in the TrainJob status, so tuning can live inside Trainer.
  3. 12:13 The speaker says trials are just TrainJobs, so they reuse runtimes, JobSet and gang scheduling, and each trial is queued by Kueue like any other.
  4. 13:04 The speaker says phase one is small on purpose, with random search, one objective, alpha feature gates that are off by default, and YAML only.
  5. 14:17 The speaker lays out the roadmap in three buckets, which are smarter search, more resiliency and a Python SDK.
