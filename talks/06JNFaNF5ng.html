Talk summary

Kai-Hsun Chen describes how Hark uses Ray, KubeRay, and MultiKueue to scale multimodal and agent workloads across Kubernetes clusters. Hao Jiang walks through the audio pipeline, from mixed CPU and GPU data processing to evaluation and high-availability model serving.

Watch this if:

you want to see how one team connects GPU admission and autoscaling to the stages of a real multimodal workload.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:09 Chen introduces a video showing what Hark's computer-use agents do.
  2. 1:32 Chen explains how KubeRay and MultiKueue work together across clusters, with autoscaling tied to application demand and admission controlled separately.
  3. 2:52 Jiang describes audio processing stages such as chunking, speech recognition, and scoring, and says Ray Data handles the mix of CPU and GPU work.
  4. 3:45 Jiang describes an internal launcher for training jobs and explains how asynchronous evaluation can add or release GPU workers as demand changes.
  5. 4:13 Jiang explains how Ray handles inference request routing and replicas while RayService supports high availability and cluster updates.
