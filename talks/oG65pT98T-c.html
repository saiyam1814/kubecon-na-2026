Talk summary

Murtuza Mukadam argues that operations teams can make better decisions by connecting the signals from their existing tools before acting on them. He uses an overnight outage to illustrate the limits of separate dashboards and says people should control how far automation can go.

Watch this if:

You want a short explanation of why more dashboards do not necessarily make incident response easier.

Key points from the speaker

  1. 0:08 Mukadam uses a 2 a.m. outage to illustrate how alerts, dashboards, and AI assessments can leave responders with plenty of tabs but no coordinated next step.
  2. 1:27 He contrasts the teams that own cost, performance, and security signals with the customer who experiences the service as a whole.
  3. 2:05 He says each tool holds only part of the answer, leaving responders to assemble the incident context themselves.
  4. 2:25 He proposes connecting existing tools into shared context so teams can start by deciding what to do, rather than spending the first part of an incident collecting evidence.
  5. 3:08 He warns that automation without enough context creates risk, and argues for giving people control over how far the automation can go.
