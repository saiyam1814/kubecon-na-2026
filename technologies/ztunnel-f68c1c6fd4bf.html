    Istio Day Opening Remarks

    Faseela Kundattil, Jackie Maertens

    The co-chairs open Istio Day, recap what they say the project shipped over the last year, and list roadmap items for the next. They ask attendees to tell the project what they want prioritized and explain how to get involved.

    5 min slot · Level not listed · Session

    Unclassified

    Watch this if: You want a four-minute recap of what Istio shipped in the last year and what its maintainers plan next.

    1. 0:59 The co-chair says Istio made four releases over the last year, with the latest a few days before, focused on functionality, compatibility and security fixes.
    2. 1:29 The co-chair lists ambient mesh improvements, including the promotion of multi-cluster for ambient and work on migration from sidecar to ambient.
