    From Pod Creation to First Packet

    Surya Seetharaman

    Surya Seetharaman traces pod networking from Kubernetes creating a sandbox to sending its first packet. She uses a kind cluster to show the network namespace, CNI setup, address assignment, and packet capture.

    30 min slot · Beginner · Session

    Kubernetes internals and runtimesNetworking and connectivity

    Watch this if: You want a practical way to trace a pod startup networking failure from its sandbox to its first outbound packet.

    1. 6:26 Seetharaman describes when the runtime creates the pause container and network namespace after the kubelet receives a pod.
    2. 8:06 Seetharaman uses crictl to identify a running pod's sandbox and explains why the pause container is not listed like an application container.
    3. 13:13 Seetharaman distinguishes the loopback setup from the primary CNI plugin and identifies kindnet as the plugin used in her demo.
    4. 20:11 Seetharaman explains how kindnet uses host-local IP address management to allocate a pod address from a configured range.
    5. 24:09 Seetharaman clears the ARP cache and captures traffic during a ping to show the address resolution and packets leaving the pod.
