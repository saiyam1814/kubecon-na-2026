    Introducing go-yaml's New Plugin System

    Ingy döt Net

    Ingy döt Net, a co-creator of YAML who now leads maintenance of go-yaml, shows the plugin system in go-yaml v4, which he says lets an application change how YAML is loaded without changing the spec. He runs live examples of plugins for importing files, aliases across documents and tab indentation, and also tours his related projects YAMLScript, YAML Schema and YAMLStar.

    30 min slot · Any Level · Session

    Platform engineering and developer experience

    Watch this if: you maintain Go software that reads YAML and have wanted imports, custom tags or other behavior the standard loader does not give you.

    1. 3:39 He runs go-yaml v4's alias data and function tag plugins live: an import tag loads a separate vars file into an anchor, so shared values no longer have to appear in the output document.
    2. 6:29 He walks through the loader's phases (read, scan, parse, compose, resolve, construct) and shows that the resolver gives every untagged node a tag, which is what the plugins hook into.
    3. 13:53 He tells how the widely used go-yaml/yaml was archived in 2025 and how, working with Dims, the Kubernetes fork's patches were folded into the new yaml/go-yaml, which he says Kubernetes now uses.
    4. 16:58 He defines a plugin as a piece of the library that people want different behavior from, moved behind an API, and says it lets the core team try ideas before they go into the spec.
    5. 25:03 He demos tab-indented YAML loading through a plugin, then says the parser itself can be swapped and shows a CSV parser he wrote in YAMLScript.
