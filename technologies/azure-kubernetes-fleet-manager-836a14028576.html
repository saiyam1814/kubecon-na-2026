    Multicluster AMA: The Community Answers

    August Simonelli, Laura Lorenz, Liqian Luo, Trevor Swanson

    Moderator August Simonelli puts audience questions about running many Kubernetes clusters to Laura Lorenz of SIG Multicluster, Liqian Luo of Azure Kubernetes Service and Trevor Swanson of Evernorth. The panelists share their views on going from one cluster to many, GitOps and scheduling, staged upgrades, self-service for developers, and where AI fits.

    30 min slot · Any Level · Panel

    Platform engineering and developer experienceNetworking and connectivityCommunity and open source adoption

    Watch this if: your cluster count grew without a plan and you want to hear how an operator, a cloud provider and the upstream SIG think about managing a fleet.

    1. 6:05 Laura Lorenz says SIG Multicluster focuses on APIs for cross-cluster problems and names the Multi-Cluster Services API, the Work API and the newer Cluster Inventory API.
    2. 8:05 Trevor Swanson advises building for a fleet of one cluster: nothing bespoke to a cluster name, label everything and make it policy-based from day one.
    3. 8:48 Liqian Luo says GitOps may suit static placement, but in her view is not enough when workloads must be placed by GPU type, topology and available capacity.
    4. 18:17 Lorenz says the SIG has focused on small composable building blocks rather than one monolithic solution, and that it could do more to explain how the pieces fit together.
    5. 23:45 Swanson argues that declarative beats clever when agents act on a fleet, and Luo says AI helps with observing and diagnosing but that letting it change clusters arbitrarily is not safe.
