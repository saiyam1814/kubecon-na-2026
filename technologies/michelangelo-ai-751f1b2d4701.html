    Built on Kubernetes. Proven at Uber Scale. Now Open Source.

    Viv Keswani

    Viv Keswani announces that Uber is open sourcing Michelangelo AI, the Kubernetes-based platform he says runs all of Uber's critical machine learning. He shares Uber's scale figures for models and predictions and asks the community for feedback and code contributions.

    3 min slot · Level not listed · Keynote

    Community and open source adoptionKubernetes internals and runtimes

    Watch this if: you want an open source, Kubernetes-based ML platform that a large company already runs in production.

    1. 0:04 Viv Keswani announces that Uber is open sourcing Michelangelo AI, the platform behind machine learning and AI at Uber.
    2. 0:43 He says Uber's models support millions of concurrent trips across 15,000 cities, and that Uber runs over 5,300 models in production at about 45 million peak predictions per second.
    3. 1:03 He says predictions must finish in milliseconds with at least four nines of reliability, and that teams expect retrained models to reach production without human oversight.
    4. 1:37 He says 100% of Uber's critical machine learning builds and runs on Michelangelo, which grew from traditional ML to generative AI to agentic AI over more than a decade.
    5. 2:25 He says Michelangelo is built on Kubernetes and brings open source projects into one developer experience, and asks for feedback and code contributions.
