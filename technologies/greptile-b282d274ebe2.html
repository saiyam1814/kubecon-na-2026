    How Podium Ships 30% of PRs with Autonomous K8s Coding Agents

    Drew Bowman

    Drew Bowman describes how Podium runs coding-agent sessions on Kubernetes and shares what the team learned about keeping those sessions recoverable. He explains why the team asks agents to attach evidence from realistic test environments and requires a human to approve their pull requests.

    30 min slot · Any Level · Session

    AI agents and automationDelivery, GitOps and release safety

    Watch this if: You are designing an internal coding-agent platform and want examples of recoverable sessions, realistic testing, and human review.

    1. 3:45 Bowman describes Podium's ORA service receiving events and handing work to an orchestrator that schedules agent sessions in pods.
    2. 5:34 Bowman explains how a separate disk-backed workspace for each session lets the team resume its work after a pod dies.
    3. 16:58 Bowman describes replacing separate staging stacks with shared team namespaces and using mirrord to route test traffic to a specific service preview.
    4. 20:39 Bowman explains the team's test evidence requirement: reproduce the failure against the unchanged service and show the result against the preview.
    5. 21:34 Bowman discusses the automated review checks Podium uses and says that changes from autonomous agents still need human approval.
