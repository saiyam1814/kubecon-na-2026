    Meshery: Your Cluster as a Knowledge Graph, Not a Pile of YAML

    Yash Sharma

    Yash Sharma, a Meshery maintainer, introduces Meshery as a cloud native manager for visualizing and collaborating on infrastructure rather than piecing it together from kubectl output. He covers multi-cluster management, policy evaluation of designs, the design catalog and integrations, and shares project news including CNCF incubation.

    5 min slot · Level not listed · Lightning Talk

    Platform engineering and developer experienceCommunity and open source adoption

    Watch this if: you want a quick overview of what Meshery does and where the project stands after incubation.

    1. 0:34 Yash Sharma describes Meshery as a cloud native manager that shows how infrastructure resources are related and connected in a GUI, instead of working it out from kubectl get.
    2. 1:15 He says Meshery has policy-based configuration management that evaluates designs deterministically, which he presents as a check on AI-generated designs.
    3. 1:41 He describes a design catalog for reusing designs that other people inside or outside an organization have already created.
    4. 2:45 He says Meshery is the fifth highest velocity CNCF project and announces that it became a CNCF incubating project this month.
    5. 3:01 He mentions a headless mesheryctl wrapper meant for use with agents and says a Meshery MCP server is coming after KubeCon.
