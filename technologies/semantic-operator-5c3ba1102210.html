    Kubeflow Graduation: Powering the Next Era of Cloud Native AI With an Open Community

    Ron Kahn, Andrey Velichkevich, Vara Bonthu, Ekin Karabulut, Manabu McCloskey

    Andrey Velichkevich announces Kubeflow's CNCF graduation and describes a project that has grown from making machine learning portable on Kubernetes into a platform for the AI lifecycle. The other speakers explain how their teams use it for distributed training and Spark workloads, and introduce a way to give agents shared definitions for business metrics.

    7 min slot · Level not listed · Keynote

    AI inference and servingCommunity and open source adoption

    1. 0:18 Velichkevich announces CNCF graduation and thanks the users, contributors, and maintainers who made it possible.
