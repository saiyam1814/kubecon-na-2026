    Salesforce Runs on Heroku: How We Empower Every Builder at Cloud Native Scale

    Brandon Schoen, Shiva Nimmagadda

    Shiva Nimmagadda describes what Salesforce needs from Heroku so employees with different levels of technical experience can deploy internal apps. Brandon Schoen explains how Heroku uses Kubernetes, Cloud Native Buildpacks, and OpenTelemetry to handle orchestration, base image updates, and observability for those builders.

    3 min slot · Level not listed · Keynote

    Platform engineering and developer experience

    Watch this if: you want a concrete example of how an internal app platform can hide deployment, patching, and observability work from builders.

    1. 0:29 Nimmagadda says Salesforce runs more than twenty thousand Heroku apps and gives examples of what sales, operations, and engineering teams build.
    2. 0:59 Nimmagadda lists requirements for taking AI-assisted code to production, including simple deployment, isolation, single sign-on, and visibility into failures.
    3. 1:42 Schoen names three CNCF projects behind Heroku and describes Kubernetes as its orchestration and isolation layer.
    4. 2:26 Schoen explains that standardizing image builds lets Heroku patch base operating systems across its fleet without rebuilding app code.
    5. 2:40 Schoen describes how Heroku collects logs, traces, and metrics and links the routing layer, platform APIs, and runtime to show a request's journey.
