    Explore TAG Workloads Foundation: Core Runtime, Batch Scheduling, and Moar

    Stephen Rust, Yuan Tang, Marlow Warnicke, Rajas Kakodkar, Paco Xu

    Five leads of the CNCF TAG Workloads Foundation explain what a technical advisory group does, what this one covers, and how its batch subproject and white paper initiatives run. They then walk through graduated, incubating and sandbox projects they place in the TAG's scope and describe how newcomers can join meetings, reviews and initiatives.

    30 min slot · Level not listed · Session

    Community and open source adoptionKubernetes internals and runtimesAccelerators and workload scheduling

    Watch this if: you maintain or use a workload, runtime or scheduling project and want to know what the TAG does and how to take part.

    1. 2:10 Stephen Rust describes a TAG as a technical advisory group that supports CNCF projects and says it is a way to scale advice across many projects with few people.
    2. 5:13 Rust says initiatives are time-bound efforts with a deliverable such as a white paper, while subprojects carry longer-term work, and that the TAG helps projects with their maturity reviews.
    3. 10:23 Marlow Warnicke describes the batch subproject's scheduling capabilities white paper, which she says aims to compare schedulers under different workload mixes, and asks for contributors.
    4. 14:15 Paco Xu lists graduated projects he places in the TAG's scope and says Karmada and Kubeflow graduated this year and KubeVirt graduated recently.
    5. 19:07 Rajas Kakodkar says no single role or expertise is needed to get involved, and suggests joining meetings, sharing pain points, demoing a project or reviewing white papers.
