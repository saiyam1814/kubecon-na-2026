    Who's Governing the GPU Lane? Policy as Code for AI Inference Traffic in Kubernetes

    Ciara Stacke, Alessandro Fael Garcia

    The speakers argue that RBAC only decides who may create an object, not what is in it, and walk through demo scenarios in which a tenant team tries to reach models or GPUs it should not use. They show Kyverno validating policies in front of NGINX Gateway Fabric and InferencePool resources blocking each attempt, and Policy Reporter listing resources created before the policies existed.

    25 min slot · Level not listed · Session

    Security, identity and policyAI inference and servingNetworking and connectivity

    Watch this if: You run shared GPU inference behind Gateway API and want admission policies for tenant isolation, model access and cost labels.

    1. 0:56 Alessandro Fael Garcia says RBAC decides who may create an object but not what is in it, and that AI inference traffic needs more granular checks.
    2. 9:04 Ciara Stacke shows a Kyverno validating policy under which only the platform team may share InferencePools through a ReferenceGrant, while tenant teams can still share services.
    3. 12:27 She says Kyverno can check each HTTPRoute against a per-team list of approved models, and then shows the route to the larger model being refused.
    4. 14:48 She requires cost center and tier labels on GPU workloads, and the demo denies a self-deployed copy of the large model.
    5. 17:10 She shows a policy that stops teams in regulated namespaces from switching their InferencePool from fail closed to fail open.
