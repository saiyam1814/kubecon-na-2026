    Cloud Native without Limits: Powering the AI Era in the Open

    Chris Aniszczyk, Jonathan Bryce, Rita Zhang, Kevin Cochrane, Robert Nishihara, Justin Boitano, Bassam Tabbara

    The opening speakers say that agents are driving inference demand and describe how cloud providers are contributing fixes from production back to open source. The session includes announcements about KubeRay and OpenShell and a ModelPlane demo that moves an inference deployment between clouds.

    Quota Isn't Enough: Topology Aware Scheduling on Modern GPU Clusters

    Amy Chen, JJ Jordan

    Amy Chen and JJ Jordan explain why GPU quota alone does not tell you whether a training job can fit within the physical network layout. They walk through Kueue topology-aware admission and share operational examples involving node replacement, preemption, and fragmented capacity.

    30 min slot · Advanced · Session

    Accelerators and workload scheduling

    Watch this if: You schedule distributed GPU training and need to reason about rack boundaries, queue delays, or stranded capacity.

    1. 3:58 The speakers compare two placements with the same GPU count and explain why an uneven split across racks makes every all-reduce round wait on slower cross-rack transfers.
    2. 8:07 The speakers show how an example 128-GPU job asks Kueue for two 64-GPU slices and how admission checks both quota and rack-level fit.
    3. 13:31 The speakers compare unconstrained, preferred, and required placement, framing the choice as a trade-off between queue time and training speed.
    4. 16:37 The speakers describe replacing one unhealthy node within the same topology so a run can resume from a checkpoint without readmitting the whole workload.
    5. 19:16 The speakers explain why Kueue can decline to preempt smaller jobs when evicting them still would not free a large enough contiguous placement.
