    Agentic AI: Let’s Build a Reference Architecture, Live? Or at Least Try!

    Ricardo Rocha

    Ricardo Rocha invites practitioners to sketch a cloud-native reference architecture for agentic AI from their own deployments. The group maps layers for gateway and inference, then discusses caller identity, privacy, and why an agent's lifetime affects how it should be run.

    30 min slot · Level not listed · Birds of a Feather

    AI agents and automationPlatform engineering and developer experience

    Watch this if: you are planning an agent platform and want to compare architecture choices with other practitioners.

    1. 6:36 Rocha shows the draft architecture and places the gateway at the entry point, LangChain around orchestration, and Langfuse in the observability layer.
    2. 10:11 An attendee says caller identity can be lost on the way to the model and suggests preserving it across the chain for attribution and tracing, mentioning SPIFFE and SPIRE.
    3. 13:06 An attendee describes identifying and tokenizing sensitive fields before sending requests to a third-party inference engine, even if the requests are already signed.
    4. 22:16 An attendee contrasts one-shot agents that are seldom reused with long-running jobs, and Rocha adds that distinction to the architecture discussion about checkpointing and resuming work.
    5. 28:34 Rocha asks teams to contribute reference architectures and says submissions should include what worked, what did not, and the custom integration work they needed.
